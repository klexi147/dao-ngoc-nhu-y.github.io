02 COMPLETED

Customer Personality Segmentation & Marketing Analytics

Segment 2,240 customers based on demographics, spending, purchasing behaviour and campaign response to support targeted marketing and customer retention.

Python K-Means scikit-learn pandas
2,240 Customers
29 Features
4 Segments

Objective

Identify meaningful customer groups based on purchasing behaviour, spending patterns and campaign response.

Data Preparation

  • Analysed 2,240 customers across 29 features.
  • Applied median imputation for missing income.
  • Created customer tenure and date-based features.
  • Applied log transformation to skewed spending and income variables.

Modelling

Applied K-Means clustering and evaluated alternative cluster structures using the elbow method and silhouette analysis. A four-cluster solution was selected as a balance between statistical separation and business interpretability.

Business Result

Identified distinct customer profiles ranging from moderate purchasers to high-value and luxury-oriented customers, supporting differentiated promotion, loyalty and personalised marketing strategies.

03 COMPLETED

Customer Behavior & Product Recommendation – Amazon Electronics

Analyse 7.8M+ product-rating interactions and develop personalised recommendation models to improve product relevance.

Python Collaborative Filtering SVD GridSearchCV
7.8M+ Interactions
0.97 RMSE
0.77 F1-score

Objective

Develop recommendation approaches based on historical customer-product interactions to support personalised product discovery.

Modelling

  • Compared rank-based recommendations, user-user collaborative filtering, item-item collaborative filtering and SVD matrix factorisation.
  • Tuned model parameters using GridSearchCV.
  • Evaluated models using RMSE, Precision@10, Recall@10 and F1-score.

Result

Selected an optimised SVD model with RMSE of approximately 0.97 and F1-score of approximately 0.77, providing a basis for personalised product recommendations.

Customer Behavior & Conversion Analytics – ExtraaLearn

Analyse 4,612 leads and build predictive models to identify conversion drivers and support lead prioritisation.

Python Decision Tree Random Forest scikit-learn
4,612 Leads
0.92 ROC-AUC
0.77 F1-score

Objective

Identify behavioural and demographic factors associated with lead conversion and develop models to support lead prioritisation.

Analysis

  • Analysed 4,612 lead records.
  • Investigated website engagement, interaction channels, occupation, profile completion and referral behaviour.
  • Addressed class imbalance and compared Decision Tree and Random Forest models.

Result

The tuned Random Forest achieved a ROC-AUC of approximately 0.92. Feature importance highlighted website engagement, first interaction channel and page views as key predictive factors.

Business Application

Findings can support lead prioritisation, website optimisation, customer targeting and marketing channel allocation.
