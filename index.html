01 ONGOING

Voice-of-Customer Analytics – Multilingual Social Listening Pipeline

Analyze multilingual consumer feedback across Germany, UK, Netherlands and the US to identify recurring pain points, product topics and feature requests.

Python YouTube Data API Claude API BERTopic Hugging Face

Scope

Multilingual consumer-generated data from Germany, UK, Netherlands and the US. More than 7,500 public YouTube records from 2023–2026.

Methodology

  • Data collection using the official YouTube Data API.
  • Deduplication, language detection and translation for cross-market comparison.
  • Relevance scoring to filter noisy consumer comments.
  • LLM-assisted extraction of pain points, praise and feature requests.
  • Topic clustering using BERTopic.

Result

Structured multilingual consumer feedback into comparable product-related themes and market-specific insights.

Data Quality & Limitations

AI outputs were audited and validation issues were investigated rather than accepted blindly. Public data and official APIs were used within data-governance constraints.

02 COMPLETED

Customer Personality Segmentation & Marketing Analytics

Segment 2,240 customers based on demographics, spending, purchasing behavior and campaign response to support targeted marketing and retention strategies.

Python pandas scikit-learn K-Means

Business Problem

Identify meaningful customer groups based on demographics, spending behavior, purchasing channels and campaign response.

Data

2,240 customer records with demographic, spending, campaign interaction, web, catalog and store purchase variables.

Methodology

  • Exploratory data analysis and distribution analysis.
  • Missing-value treatment and feature preparation.
  • Feature engineering including customer tenure and total spending.
  • Log transformation and scaling.
  • K-Means clustering and cluster profiling.

Business Result

Identified four meaningful customer segments ranging from moderate-value customers to high-value and luxury-oriented customers. Segments were translated into targeted marketing and retention recommendations.

03 COMPLETED

Customer Behavior & Product Recommendation – Amazon Electronics

Analyze 7.8M+ product-rating interactions and develop personalized recommendation models to improve recommendation relevance.

Python Collaborative Filtering SVD GridSearchCV

Business Problem

Use historical customer-product interactions to identify customer preferences and generate personalized product recommendations.

Dataset

7.8M+ Amazon product-rating interactions containing user, product, rating and timestamp information.

Models

  • Rank-based recommendation.
  • User-user collaborative filtering.
  • Item-item collaborative filtering.
  • SVD matrix factorization.
  • Hyperparameter tuning using GridSearchCV.

Result

The optimized SVD model achieved an RMSE of approximately 0.97 and an F1-score of approximately 0.77 and was selected for personalized recommendation use cases.

04 COMPLETED

Customer Behavior & Conversion Analytics – ExtraaLearn

Analyze 4,612 leads and build predictive models to identify conversion drivers and support lead prioritization.

Python pandas Decision Tree Random Forest

Business Problem

Identify customer and behavioral factors associated with lead conversion and support better prioritization of marketing leads.

Data

4,612 lead records covering demographic, engagement, interaction channel, profile completion and conversion information.

Analysis

  • Exploratory data analysis.
  • Categorical encoding and preprocessing.
  • Decision Tree classification.
  • Random Forest classification.
  • Model evaluation using F1-score, recall and ROC-AUC.

Result

The tuned Random Forest achieved a ROC-AUC of approximately 0.92. Model findings highlighted website engagement, interaction channel and profile completion as important conversion-related factors.
