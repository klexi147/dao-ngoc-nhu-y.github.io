Business Problem

Consumer feedback is distributed across multilingual online channels and contains substantial noise. The objective is to structure this unstructured feedback into comparable consumer insights across markets.

Approach

  • Collected 7,500+ public YouTube records from 2023–2026.
  • Applied deduplication, language detection, translation and relevance filtering.
  • Used LLM-assisted processing to structure comments into pain points, praise and feature requests.
  • Applied topic modelling and clustering to identify recurring consumer themes.
Key focus: Building a transparent and auditable pipeline for converting multilingual unstructured consumer feedback into structured insights.
